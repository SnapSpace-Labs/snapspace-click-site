ARCHITECTURE

A control plane between AI intent and real-world consequence.

SnapSpace keeps policy, decision, authority, execution, interruption, effect truth and reconciliation separate so one stage cannot silently impersonate another.

01

POLICY

Define the rule, risk boundary and authority source.

02

RUNTIME CONTROL

Make the rule executable at the point where action can occur.

03

DECISION

Return ALLOW / DENY / ESCALATE against current state and authority.

04

BOUNDED AUTHORITY

Bind the exact principal, action, material, state, scope and expiry.

05

EXECUTION

Use stable attempt identity and refuse stale, rebound or duplicate work.

06

STOP

Interrupt active consequential work through a separate authenticated path.

07

EFFECT TRUTH

Keep acknowledgement, effect and responsibility transfer distinct.

08

RECONCILIATION

Resolve UNKNOWN_EFFECT without blind retry or invented success.

09

EVIDENCE

Preserve the exact chain of what was authorised, attempted, applied and resolved.

NORMAL DECISION

ALLOW / DENY / ESCALATE

Only ALLOW can create a bounded grant. DENY creates no authority. ESCALATE means the current authority cannot safely decide.

ACTIVE INTERRUPT

STOP is separate.

STOP revokes remaining authority in scope and contains further effects where technically possible. It is not rollback and not a fourth normal verdict.

FEDERATION

SHARE STATE, NOT AUTHORITY.

Independent domains may exchange purpose-, freshness- and provenance-bound state. The receiving domain still makes its own admission decision and creates its own authority.

CLAIM CEILING

The architecture defines control semantics. Each concrete integration must separately qualify its persistence, transport, physical-safe-state and external-effect behaviour.
