SNAPSPACE LABS

Independent engineering for consequential AI control.

SnapSpace Labs is an independent research and engineering project founded by Jason Cousins to build control infrastructure for AI systems that can take consequential action.

MISSION

Make consequential AI controllable at the point where intent becomes action.

SnapSpace Labs develops control infrastructure for systems that can call tools, move money, operate infrastructure, coordinate across independent domains or control autonomous systems.

FOUNDER

Jason Cousins

Founder and independent builder of SnapSpace. The programme combines software engineering, qualification, simulation and applied research across AI control, distributed systems and autonomous-system boundaries.

OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Evidence before claim.

Qualified results keep their environment and limitation. Negative results remain visible. Research is not promoted into capability until it passes the relevant qualification gate.
