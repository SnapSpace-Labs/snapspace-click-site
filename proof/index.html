WHY THESE NUMBERS MATTER

The evidence is organised by scale, adversarial depth, environment and reproducibility.

SnapSpace does not manufacture one giant test total by adding inherited regressions and repeated runs together. Each figure keeps its own environment and claim ceiling.

Repeated qualification

Critical suites are rerun independently rather than represented by one successful execution.

Adversarial cases

Stale authority, replay, malformed inputs, changed material, forged STOP, partial effects and lost-response states are tested explicitly where applicable.

Deterministic replay

The two-domain fabric records REPLAY_MATCH plus an independent verifier result of VERIFIED / EVIDENCE_GRAPH_VALID.

Sealed evidence

Canonical convergence records 48/48 PASS plus 9/9 sealed-artifact verification; hashes bind frozen evidence to exact candidate material.

Real accelerator execution

A sealed real two-GPU CUDA/NCCL distributed-training qualification closes part of the gap between pure SIL and actual accelerator execution on one physical host.
