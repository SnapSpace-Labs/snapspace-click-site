AI CONTROL & GOVERNANCE INFRASTRUCTURE

Policies become controls. Approvals gate actions. Authority stays bounded through execution.

SnapSpace governs the path from policy and approval through authority, execution, STOP, effect, reconciliation and evidence.

NORMAL DECISION

Proposal is not authority.

PROPOSE→DECIDE→ALLOW / DENY / ESCALATE

Only an effective ALLOW may create a bounded GRANT. DENY means no grant. ESCALATE means the current authority cannot safely decide.

ALLOWED PATH

Authority remains separate from proof of effect.

ALLOW→GRANT→EXECUTE→OUTCOME→RECEIPT

A grant is pre-commit authority. Execution, acknowledgement, effect and responsibility transfer remain separate truths.

SEPARATE INTERRUPT

STOP is not a fourth decision.

ACTIVE EXECUTION→STOP→CONTAIN→SAFE STATE→RECONCILE→STOP RECEIPT

STOP revokes remaining authority in scope and prevents further effects where technically possible. Safe state is domain-specific.

FEDERATION RULE

SHARE STATE, NOT AUTHORITY.

Domains may exchange bounded, purpose/freshness/provenance-bound state. The receiving domain keeps its own admission and consequential authority.

TRUTH MODEL

Do not collapse the evidence chain.

Decision → grant/authority → durable attempt → execution → acknowledgement → effect → responsibility transfer → reconciliation.

CLAIM CEILING

No universal federation, physical safe-state, production, national, clinical or regulatory claim is implied by the bounded software architecture.
