APPLICATIONS

Where the control pattern can be applied.

Applications are use patterns beneath capabilities, not renamed internal programmes or unsupported product claims.

AI agents & tool execution

Scoped authority, attempt identity, replay protection, STOP and receipts.

Machine purchasing & transactions

Purchase authority, budget boundaries, uncertain payment handling and delivery/clearing evidence.

Institutional & healthcare handoffs

Explicit release, bounded projection, receiver admission, responsibility-transfer truth and reconciliation.

Compute & data-centre workloads

Workload admission, distributed execution, STOP and evidence with SIL/real-GPU ceilings kept explicit.

Critical-infrastructure information

Provenance, canonical state and bounded advisory/API consumption; no implied physical actuation.

Autonomous physical systems

Contextual authority, bounded action, STOP and recovery; current proof remains simulator/software-bounded.

Cross-domain cooperation

Bounded projections, semantic interchange, independent admission and retained local authority.

Human-governed automation

Analysis/proposal, escalation, exact human decision, fresh revalidation and execution.

Disconnected / mission systems

Local authority, degraded communications, finite-resource operation, STOP and reconciliation; software/SIL ceiling unless stronger evidence exists.

CLAIM CEILING

Applications describe where qualified control patterns can be used. They are not claims of production deployment, institutional adoption, certification or field qualification.
